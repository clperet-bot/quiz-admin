🌸 C — S'entraîner à structurer sa critique

Complète cette critique avec les bons connecteurs

, I think this movie is very good.

, the action scenes are amazing and the main actor is very convincing.

, the middle part of the movie feels a little too long.

In my opinion, this is one of the best movies of the year.

✏️ Entraînement final : écris ta critique

Overall, I think this movie is exciting.
First of all, the story about a hero nobody remembers is very original.
However, some parts of the story are a little confusing.
In my opinion, this movie is worth watching, especially for Spider-Man fans.
